How it works: your strength = (ranged troops × ranged strength × ranged malus + melee troops × melee strength × melee malus) × (1 + commander CY% + tools + the fixed +60% courtyard-assault bonus) + Giant Slayer. The commander CY% is the sum of your equipment, skill/legend and research boosts on the card. Per-troop strength bakes in your troop boosts (2,440 for an L10 mead-fed elite — tweak under Advanced). The boss's strength = its real CY units × their defence × (1 + its CY defence bonus) — dormant spores and rift-eggs add zero defence but still count toward the kill total. Fungal is calibrated on 10 reports (rms 13%); Ashen on 1; Necromancer is uncalibrated — watch its malus: it cuts ranged at L11 and melee at L12+ by 60%.
