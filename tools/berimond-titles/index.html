🐻 Berimond Gallantry Titles

Berimond runs its own title ladder, separate from glory — the game calls them gallantry titles. The first twenty are bought with gallantry points; the last seven are bought with a position, which is why the top of Berimond is a race and not a grind. Below: the live faction board while a run is on, the full ladder, and what your own points are worth.

Live faction board

Straight off the game servers: the points scored in the run that is on now, which reset when it ends. This is not the gallantry total the titles are built on. Search a rank number or a name to jump.

Two different numbers, and only one of them is public. The board ranks players on the points they have scored in the current run. Titles are bought with gallantry points, a separate total that carries over between runs and decays — and the game never sends another player's gallantry total or gallantry rank to your client. The only thing it does send about someone else's titles is the prefix and suffix they have selected for display, which is the "title shown" column. A player who holds Claw King but displays a glory title looks, from the outside, like a player with no gallantry title at all.

Which title do my points buy?

Your gallantry total is on no leaderboard — read it off the Gallantry titles panel in game, which also shows your position. The points carry over between Berimond runs but bleed away every day, so the question is not only what you have reached, it is what you can hold.

The gallantry ladder

How the ladder works

  • Two currencies for one ladder. Steps 1–20 unlock at a fixed gallantry-point total. Steps 21–27 unlock at a position instead — top 100, 75, 50, 30, 20, 10 and finally first. No amount of points buys those; somebody has to be behind you.
  • Points decay daily. Each title carries its own loss rate, rising to 7% a day at the top. Holding a title is a separate job from reaching it — if a day's decay drops you under the threshold, the title goes with it.
  • The top thresholds move. The game refreshes the points needed for the ranked positions every 12 hours, and treats them as approximate even then. The live board above is the real answer to "what does top 10 cost today".
  • Four separate boards. Ursidae (blue) and Gerbrandt (red) each get a level 40–69 board and a level 70 board, so a position is a position within your own list.
  • You keep the bonus either way. Every title you hold applies its effect whether or not you have it selected for display — selecting only changes the name other lords see. Which is exactly why you cannot tell from outside who holds what: most people leave a glory title on, and the gallantry one they hold is never sent to anyone else's client.
