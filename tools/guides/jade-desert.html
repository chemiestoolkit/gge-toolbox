🏜️ The Jade Desert

Event Beyond the Horizon Alliance ~9 min read

Your whole alliance lands in the desert around one Citadel. Raid fortresses, trade at oases, grow your reach, then defend what you built. The alliance with the most Restored Jade Statuettes wins.

This is the new Beyond the Horizon mode. The older Beyond the Horizon guide covers city states, capitals and influence. None of that applies here, so don't mix the two sets of advice.

The short version

  • Phase 1 (48 hours): hit palace fortresses, craft, trade at oases, and put your Ancient statuettes into the Citadel.
  • Phase 2: castles and citadels can be hit from anywhere. Defend your Citadel and go after rivals.
  • Spending on the Citadel doesn't cost points. Spent statuettes still convert when the event ends.
  • Don't convert early. It's 4:1 early and 1:1 at the end.

Getting in

Your main castle needs a Refinery, a Toolsmith and a Siege Workshop at level 4 (player level 70 and legendary level 390). Your whole alliance stays together as one team of up to 65, and you get 6 hours of peace on arrival.

The copy is made once, when you enter. After that the two castles are separate. Rubies you buy in the desert move to your main account when the event ends.

First hour

  • Equip your best look items. Phase 2 is all PvP and this is the biggest lever on it.
  • Fix your brewery. Production arrives at 0.
  • Check your construction items came across.
  • Start the Toolsmith and Refinery. They run 5 hours a batch, so keep them busy from hour one.
Check whether it's a beta run. In a beta, rewards differ, ruby purchases are disabled and nothing you earn carries over.

Three rules decide the event:

  1. At the end, every Ancient converts 1:1, including those spent on Citadel upgrades. A statuette in the Citadel is parked, not gone.
  2. Early conversion is 4:1. Only leaders and deputies can do it. It takes Ancients from every member in proportion to their stock, so save it for statuettes you're about to lose.
  3. Losing a Citadel level is the real loss. The attacker loots part of what that level cost, and the rest is shared among your alliance's top event players.

So the plan isn't to hoard or convert. Park everything in the Citadel, then defend it.

The map shows who's carrying. A castle's Ancient hoard appears as a rough band, with thresholds at 50, 250, 1,250 and 5,000. Use it to pick phase 2 targets, and remember it works on you too.

Phase 1: the economic race

For the first 48 hours your armies can only strike inside your Citadel's radius. That goes for fortresses, oases and players. Everything else is behind the Jade fog.

You can only hit a rival once your range reaches their borders. On our map that was Citadel level 6. 🏯 Citadel Planner

The loop

  1. Raid palace fortresses. Jadeite, Varnish and Silk fortresses each drop their material plus Ancient statuettes. Travel costs half.
  2. Craft at the Toolsmith. Jade Blades make melee attackers, Jade Arrowheads ranged attackers, Silk Coats melee defenders and Silk Veils ranged defenders.
  3. Trade at oases for troops and Restored statuettes.
  4. Put your Ancients in the Citadel. More radius brings more fortresses and oases into reach.
Three things multiply the loop. Send Dune Carriers to oases, since they carry 100 loot against 50 for Jade troops. Put a Jade Idol in the support slot on fortress hits for +10%, +15% or +20% Ancient. And craft before you trade: crafted goods need 10 loot capacity per troop, raw mixes need 40.

Fortresses and oases go on an 8-hour cooldown when used up and return one tier higher. Finish them off rather than leaving half-used ones around, because that's how the alliance scales.

🐪 Oasis Trades 🏰 Palace Fortresses ⚒️ Crafting

Pick the 5-hour recipe. The 1-hour version uses the same materials and also costs 100 rubies. Only choose it when you're deliberately paying for speed.

Phase 2: borders down

Phase 2 starts at 48 hours. On runs with the early trigger it can start sooner, once 85% of at least 50 alliances reach Citadel level 4. That's a server-wide switch and separate from your own reach. You get a warning first: 1 hour on the timer, 50 minutes on the early trigger.

Castles and citadels drop out of the fog, so you can hit any of them at any Citadel level. Fortresses and oases stay inside your own radius, so the Citadel keeps mattering until the end.

Defending the Citadel

  • A lost defence drops it one level. The attacker takes a cut of that level's statuettes and the rest goes to your top players.
  • After an attack the Citadel gets a 6-hour protection window (listed for levels 1–8).
Upgrading during protection ends the protection. If you've just been hit, wait for the shield to run out before levelling back up.

Attacking

Ancients can be stolen from enemy castles as well as citadels, and most alliances are sitting on big piles by now. Use the hoard bands to pick targets.

Alliance funds

Twelve bonuses are bought with rubies from alliance funds. Maxing all seven permanent ones costs 6,435,000, so order matters.

Start with the cheap four. Ancient statuette loot, Restored from trades, oasis trade units and secure storage cost 330,000 each to max, against 1,705,000 for each wall, gate or moat line. For a coordinated fortress push, +25% fortress loot for 3 hours costs 20,000, one resource at a time.

Alliance briefing

Goal: most Restored statuettes wins. Restored can't be looted. Ancient can.

Setup: equip your best look items, fix your brewery (it starts at 0), and keep the Toolsmith and Refinery running. Always pick the 5-hour recipe.

Phase 1 (48 h): hit palace fortresses with a Jade Idol. Craft, then trade at oases with Dune Carriers. Finish fortresses and oases off so they return a tier higher.

Hand your Ancients to the Citadel. It doesn't cost us points. Don't ask for early conversion, it's 4:1 against 1:1 at the end.

Reach: we can only hit what's inside our Citadel's circle. On our map, neighbours came into reach at level 6. Watch who's one upgrade from reaching us.

Phase 2: anyone can hit any castle or citadel. Defending the Citadel is the whole game. After a hit it's protected for 6 hours, so don't upgrade during that window.
