🏕️ Rift Camp Quick Guide

Event Rift Raid Camps Tools ~4 min read

A quick rundown of each camp — what each one costs you, which tools it needs, and the order to hit it in.

A community guide by GibbyofOz, in his own words.

Why the setup keeps changing

The Rift event is designed to make it hard to use bots, as the attack setups change as each level progresses through stages. Each level is divided into stages, at the end of which an action is triggered depending on the camp and stage.

That is the whole reason a single saved attack preset will not carry you through a level — what the camp does at the end of stage 2 is not what it does at the end of stage 4, and the two camps that bank dormant units punish you for finding out late.

Tools

Ladders, rams, shields and weighted nets will work for any camp, but certain tools are specific to a camp and will not work in the other camps. Specific tools are covered in each camp section below.

Where to buy them. The shop in the rift camp sells tools for rift coins or rubies, and the master blacksmith has rift tools in the Imperial Ducats section. Full shop stock & prices →

🧟 Zombie camp

Pros. This camp gives the most time for walls down and is the easiest to understand.

Cons. A percentage of the troops you lose here come back as corrupted subjects. This isn't noticeable at low levels, but slows down progress as we go higher levels.

Zombie tools. Graveyard potions come in a 5% and 15% type to reduce zombie infection rate. There are no time tools for this camp.

Strategy. For the early stages of this camp we send ranged attackers. The courtyard makeup changes to all ranged troops, usually somewhere in stage 3, so at that point we switch to sending melee attackers.

🍄 Mycelial Sovereign (aka Spore) camp

Pros. This camp has the weakest defenders and therefore leaves us with the least losses.

Cons. This camp has the shortest wall reset times. The wall resets for this camp add dormant troops to the reserve. If these troops are still present once stage 5 is reached they transform into a stronger courtyard defender.

Mycelial tools.

ToolWhat it does
Sporebane GrenadeRemoves dormant spore maw
Sporebane Flintlock GunRemoves dormant spore cyst
Sporeguard Chrono toolsAdd extra time to either left flank, right flank, gate or all sides depending on type
Strategy. Sporebane Grenade and/or Flintlock Gun tools must be deployed after stage 1 but before stage 5.

🐉 Ashen Tyrant (aka Dragon) camp

Pros. This camp gives the highest rift point scores at higher levels.

Cons. This is the toughest camp, the most complicated and takes the longest to complete a level. The reserve contains Dormant Rift Eggs, which become Dormant Rift Wyrmlings, which become Lesser Rift Dragons, which become Greater Rift Dragons. A percentage of these are transformed every hit on the camp until there are no more dormant units left.

Dormant Rift Egg → Dormant Rift Wyrmling → Lesser Rift Dragon → Greater Rift Dragon

Ashen tools.

ToolWhat it does
Magmatic GrenadeRemoves rift eggs
Magmatic Flintlock GunRemoves dormant Rift-Wyrmlings
Magmatic Chrono toolsAdd extra time to either left flank, right flank, gate or all sides depending on type
Strategy. If the dormant units are not cleared quickly, the reserve will keep replenishing the courtyard and we will be hitting the camp for a lengthy amount of time. The earliest hits should be using Magmatic Grenade and Gun tools to wipe the dormant units before they can be active.
